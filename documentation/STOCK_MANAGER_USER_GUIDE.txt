CITY COSMETICS POS - SYSTEM DOCUMENTATION
USER ROLE: INVENTORY & STOCK MANAGER

1. YOUR ROLE IN THE SYSTEM
The Inventory & Stock Manager is responsible for product cataloging, stock receiving, barcode label generation, and batch/expiry tracking for all cosmetic products in the store.

2. CORE WORKFLOWS
- Product Intake & Registration: Record new cosmetics inventory batches upon arrival, including supplier cost, quantity, category, and expiry dates.
- Barcode Generation: Print and attach unique barcode labels to cosmetic packages and display shelves.
- Stock Transfer: Manage transfers between store branches or stockrooms.
- Stock Audit & Reconciliation: Conduct regular stock takes and reconcile physical inventory with POS numbers.

3. ETHICAL USAGE GUIDELINES
- Accuracy: Record exact quantities, cost prices, and expiration dates.
- Expiry Protection: Monitor shelf life to prevent selling expired cosmetic items.
- Transparency: Record any damaged, expired, or lost stock accurately.
