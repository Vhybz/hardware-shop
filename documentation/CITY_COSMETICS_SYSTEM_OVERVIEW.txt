========================================================================
CITY COSMETICS POS - PROJECT OVERVIEW
========================================================================

1. CORE PURPOSE
---------------
City Cosmetics POS is an end-to-end management system for cosmetic shops
and beauty retail enterprises. It automates inventory control, product batch
tracking, barcode label scanning, and high-speed Point-of-Sale (POS) with
integrated debt management and sales reporting.


2. TECHNICAL STACK
------------------
* Frontend: Flutter (SDK ^3.11.5)
* State Management: flutter_riverpod (using riverpod_generator)
* Backend: Supabase (Postgres, Realtime, Auth, Storage)
* Local Data/Offline: Hive (Sync Queue), Drift (SQLite persistence)
* Hardware: printing & pdf packages (Thermal labels/Receipts)
* Notifications: Arkesel SMS Gateway Integration


3. KEY FEATURES
---------------
* Offline-First Sync Engine (lib/services/offline_sync_service.dart):
  Validation of the Hive-based queue and conflict resolution when
  device reconnects with multiple pending updates.

* Point-of-Sale & Inventory:
  - Cosmetic Inventory & Barcode Tracking
  - Debt/Credit Customer Management
  - Automated Sales Summaries & Receipts


4. DATABASE ARCHITECTURE (Key Entities)
---------------------------------------
* products: Cosmetic item catalog & stock levels.
* sales: Transaction records and receipt generation.
* stock_transfers: Branch-to-branch movement auditing.
