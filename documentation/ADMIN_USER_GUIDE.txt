CITY COSMETICS POS - SYSTEM DOCUMENTATION
USER ROLE: ADMINISTRATOR (OWNER/MANAGER)

1. YOUR ROLE IN THE SYSTEM
The Administrator is the overseer of the entire business ecosystem. You are responsible for the financial health, staff accountability, and strategic growth of City Cosmetics. Your portal provides high-level analytics and control over all workstations.

2. CORE WORKFLOWS
- Staff Management: Approve new account registrations, assign primary and secondary roles, and manage permissions.
- Inventory Control: Set the retail, wholesale, and cost prices for all cosmetic products. Monitor stock levels and define low-stock thresholds.
- Financial Oversight: Review the Dashboard for real-time Gross and Net Profit. Monitor the "Business Expenses" ledger to ensure operational costs are controlled.
- Product Analytics: Monitor product turnover and sales velocity across categories.
- Debt Management: Monitor the "Debt Tracker" to ensure customer balances are being collected and verified.
- AI Assistance: Use the Sparkles icon on your dashboard to ask the AI Assistant for business advice, sales analysis, or system help.

3. ETHICAL USAGE GUIDELINES
- Data Integrity: Never manipulate sales or expense records to misrepresent the business's financial status.
- Fairness: Ensure staff roles and salary configurations are managed transparently.
- Privacy: Protect customer contact information found in the Customer Directory.
- Accountability: Use the "Audit Logs" to verify actions fairly.

4. SYSTEM MAINTENANCE
- Regularly review the "System Settings" to ensure branch locations and contact details are up to date.
- Perform exports of "Daily Sales Reports" and "Expense Ledgers" for accounting and tax compliance.
